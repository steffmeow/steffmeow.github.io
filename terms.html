AOR Radar Backups

Terms of use

Last updated: September 15, 2026.

AOR Radar Backups is a private backup application for its owner, not a service offered to the public. Access to its Google Drive connection is controlled by the owner's Google Account consent.

The application is intended to upload encrypted license-server backups to the owner's Google Drive. The owner is responsible for maintaining the backup decryption material separately, checking backups, and keeping their Google Account and server secure. A successful upload alone does not guarantee that a backup can be restored.

The owner can stop using the connection at any time by revoking the application's Google Account access. Existing uploaded files remain in the owner's Drive until the owner removes them.

Questions may be directed through the maintainer's GitHub profile.
