AOR Radar Backups

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026.

AOR Radar Backups is a personal tool used by its owner to back up the AOR Radar license server. It is not offered as a public service.

Google Drive access

When the owner authorizes the application, it requests the Google Drive drive.file scope. This lets the tool create and manage files that it creates in the owner's Drive, or files the owner explicitly opens with the app. It does not request unrestricted access to all Drive files.

Data handled

The tool prepares license-server backup data on the owner's server, encrypts it with age, and uploads only the encrypted archive to the owner's Google Drive. Google Drive receives the encrypted archive and its file metadata, such as name, size and upload time. The tool does not upload the decryption password or private age identity to Drive. The owner may separately store recovery material using their own choices; that is outside this tool's Drive connection.

Use, storage and sharing

Google authorization tokens are stored on the owner's server for the backup connection and used only to upload or manage the tool's backup files. Backup data is not sold or used for advertising. The tool does not share Drive files with other people or services. Google Drive stores the uploaded ciphertext under the owner's Google account, subject to Google's own terms and settings.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke the application's Google access from their Google Account and delete uploaded backup files from Drive. Revoking access stops future Drive operations by the tool, but does not automatically delete files already uploaded. The owner also controls backup copies retained on their own server.

Contact

For questions about this personal application, contact its maintainer through the GitHub profile.
